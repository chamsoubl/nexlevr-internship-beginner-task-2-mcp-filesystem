# Customer Feedback Analysis

## Scope and Sample Size

This analysis is based on **3 feedback responses** (`feedback_01.txt` to `feedback_03.txt`) from the Customer-Feedback folder. This is a very small sample, so the findings below are **indicative only** and should not be treated as representative of all customers. Where an issue appears in only one response, it is flagged as such.

---

## 1. What Customers Are Satisfied With

| Area | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ease of ordering | "The ordering website is easy to use." | Chamsou |
| Website design | "The website design is attractive." | Ahmed |
| Payment process | "The payment process is simple." | Ahmed |
| Overall service | "I am satisfied with the service." | Mohamed |
| Pricing | "The product prices are reasonable." | Mohamed |

All three customers gave some positive feedback, mainly about the website experience (usability, design, payment) and pricing.

---

## 2. Main Complaints and Recurring Issues

Each complaint below was raised by **one customer only**, so no issue is confirmed as recurring across the sample.

| Issue | Raised by |
|---|---|
| Delivery sometimes takes too long | Chamsou |
| Problems when using the website on a mobile phone | Ahmed |
| Customer support is slow to respond to questions | Mohamed |

**Feature request:** Chamsou asked for real-time order tracking.

---

## 3. Common Themes

- **Website experience is generally positive.** Ease of use, design and payment were all praised. The one website criticism concerns mobile use.
- **Speed and responsiveness are the weak point.** Two complaints (delivery time and support response time) concern how long things take. This is the closest thing to a pattern, but it rests on only two responses.
- **Every response mixes praise with one complaint.** No customer was purely negative or purely positive.
- **Order visibility.** The request for real-time tracking is related to the delivery complaint, though it comes from the same single customer.

---

## 4. Recommendations

These follow directly from the feedback above and should be validated with more data.

1. **Investigate delivery times.** Review current delivery performance and identify where delays occur. Consider communicating realistic delivery estimates at checkout.
2. **Consider real-time order tracking.** This would address both the stated request and the delivery frustration by giving customers visibility.
3. **Test and fix the mobile experience.** Check the website on common phones and browsers to find the specific problems Ahmed encountered. The feedback does not say what they were, so follow up with the customer if possible.
4. **Improve customer support response times.** Review how questions are currently handled, and consider setting a response-time target or sending an automatic acknowledgement.
5. **Protect what works.** Keep the simple ordering flow, attractive design, easy payment and reasonable pricing while making changes.
6. **Collect more feedback.** With only 3 responses, gather more (for example, a short post-order survey) before making major decisions, so that real patterns can be separated from one-off comments.

---

## Limitations

- Sample of 3 responses; no statistical conclusions can be drawn.
- Feedback is short and lacks detail (for example, no delivery times, device types or dates).
- No information on customer type, order history or timeframe.
